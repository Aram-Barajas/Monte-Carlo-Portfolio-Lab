Interpret the scenarios carefully

These outcomes depend on the selected history and model assumptions. Percentile bands are simulated scenario ranges, not confidence intervals or guarantees. Loss frequency counts paths ending below nominal contributed capital after modeled trading costs; tax and inflation effects are excluded from that metric. None of these models captures all future market or estimation uncertainty.
